05 — Questions

Straight
Answers

The final score is 50% TF-IDF cosine similarity (how semantically close your resume is to the job's full text) and 50% skill overlap (what fraction of the job's required skills appear in your resume). No black boxes, no AI hand-waving — just document geometry.
Plain text (.txt, .md) and Word documents (.docx). The .docx parser runs on the server with zero external dependencies — it reads the raw document XML directly.
Treat the missing-skills list as a prioritised learning plan: it is literally the set of tools the role expects that your resume doesn't mention. Close the biggest gaps first — they raise your skill-overlap score directly.
